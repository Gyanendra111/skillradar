Methodology

Make the evidence inspectable.

SkillRadar should help people make better decisions, not manufacture certainty. Every published insight needs a traceable source, a defined cohort, and a clear limitation.

Collection

Collect job postings only from permitted sources and record the source, role title, location, experience band, publication date, and application URL. Respect each source's terms and rate limits.

Normalisation

Map aliases such as spreadsheet tool names to a controlled skill vocabulary. Keep the original wording so users can inspect the evidence behind a grouped skill.

Analysis

Separate advertised demand from learning advice and application readiness. Do not turn these different questions into one score. Report cohort size and freshness with every live analysis.

Limitations

Job postings reflect advertised requirements. They can omit real hiring criteria, repeat duplicate roles, or overstate a preferred skill. The platform is guidance, not a hiring guarantee.
